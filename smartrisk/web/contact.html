Contact

We’d like to hear from you.

Whether something went wrong during a scan, you have an idea for the product, or you found a security issue, send us a message. Clear details help us get back to you faster.

How to reach us

General support Questions about your account, scanning, or a report. support@smartrisk.io
Security reports Tell us about a security problem or a result you believe is unsafe. security@smartrisk.io
Product feedback Suggestions that could make SmartRisk clearer, faster, or more useful. feedback@smartrisk.io

What to include in a support message

A contract address, the network you were using if you know it, and a short description of what happened are usually enough to start. For a scan issue, a screenshot or the scan result link can also save a lot of back-and-forth.

Security reports

For security issues, please avoid posting sensitive details publicly. Send the affected page or endpoint, steps to reproduce the problem, and anything that helps us understand the impact. We appreciate responsible disclosure and will review the report as soon as we can.

One important note

SmartRisk reports are analysis results, not a guarantee that a token is safe or unsafe. If you are reporting a disagreement with a result, include the contract and the exact part of the report that looks wrong so we can investigate it properly.
