Privacy Policy

How SmartRisk handles your information

We keep this policy straightforward. SmartRisk needs a small amount of information to run scans, maintain accounts, send verification emails, and keep the service reliable. We do not need your personal information just to let you scan a contract.

1. Information you provide

If you create an account, we collect your email address and a password-derived value used to authenticate you. We do not store your password in plain text. We also keep basic account information such as when the account was created and when it was last used.

2. Information from scans

When you run a scan, SmartRisk receives the contract address you submit and the information needed to process the analysis. Scan jobs and their results are stored in the service database so the report can be completed, retrieved, and the system can recover from interrupted work.

Contract addresses and on-chain activity are public blockchain information. SmartRisk may query public blockchain data and external market-data or infrastructure services to perform the analysis.

3. Email and verification

We use Resend to send account verification, password-reset, and other transactional emails. Your email address is shared with that provider only as needed to deliver those messages. SmartRisk does not use the verification email process to send unrelated marketing messages.

4. Cookies and sessions

When you sign in, SmartRisk uses a session cookie so you can stay signed in between requests. The session token is designed to be used by the application and is stored in a protected browser cookie rather than in the page's JavaScript storage. You can sign out to revoke the active session.

5. How we use information

  • To create and secure user accounts.
  • To run, store, and display contract risk scans.
  • To send verification and password-reset emails you request.
  • To monitor reliability, recover failed jobs, and protect the service from abuse.
  • To investigate technical or security problems when necessary.

6. What we do not do

We do not require an account simply to run a scan. We do not store plain-text passwords. We also do not describe a scan result as proof that a project is legitimate or fraudulent; the service is intended to surface technical risk signals and supporting evidence.

7. Data retention

SmartRisk keeps operational account and scan records for as long as they are needed to provide and maintain the service. We do not currently promise a fixed deletion timeline for scan records. As the product grows, retention rules may become more specific and will be reflected here.

8. Your choices

You can use the scanner without creating an account. If you have an account and want help with your stored account information, email us at support@smartrisk.io. We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before making changes or providing account information.

9. Changes to this policy

SmartRisk may update this policy when the service changes. When we make a meaningful change, we will update the date below and revise the wording on this page. We encourage you to check this page from time to time rather than relying on an old copy.
