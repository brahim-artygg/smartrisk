Methodology

Risk signals are stronger when the evidence agrees.

SmartRisk uses multiple analysis layers because contract risk is rarely visible from one source alone.

Static analysis

We inspect contract structure and known risk patterns, including ownership controls, upgradeability, privileged functions, trading restrictions, minting and burning behavior, and other implementation details.

State-fork analysis

Where the required chain data is available, SmartRisk can reproduce the contract state in a forked environment and test important trading paths. This helps expose sell restrictions, transfer failures, dynamic fees, and other behavior that source-code inspection alone may miss.

On-chain intelligence

Liquidity, holders, transfers, deployer activity, contract relationships, and market observations add context around the code. These signals are treated as evidence, not as proof of intent.

Correlation and verdicts

The final report combines the available signals. Strong evidence can trigger a hard verdict, while incomplete evidence is represented as unknown or limited coverage. The goal is to be explicit about what the system knows and what it does not.

Important.

The methodology is designed to find risk signals, not to predict future price or guarantee a project's behavior.
