About SmartRisk

A clearer way to look at contract risk.

SmartRisk was built for the moment before a transaction, when a contract address tells you very little and you want a practical answer about what the code and on-chain behavior are actually showing.

What SmartRisk does

SmartRisk combines several kinds of evidence instead of relying on a single warning flag. It can inspect contract code, check important permissions, simulate trading behavior, look at liquidity and holder patterns, and bring those signals together into one report.

Why it is built this way

A token can look ordinary while hiding a sell restriction, unusual owner powers, a fee that changes later, or liquidity that is much thinner than it first appears. No single check catches every case. SmartRisk is designed to make the different pieces visible in one place.

What SmartRisk is not

SmartRisk is not a promise that a token is safe, and it does not label a project as fraudulent simply because one signal looks unusual. The service reports technical evidence and risk indicators so you can make your own decision.
